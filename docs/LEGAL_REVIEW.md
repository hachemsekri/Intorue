# Items for review by a Tunisian lawyer

The app contains **placeholders only**. No final legal text has been written.

## Personal data
1. **Loi organique n° 2004-63** (personal data protection): is a declaration to, or an authorization from, the **INPDP** required before processing geolocation data? For which account types?
2. **Legal basis and notice** for:
   - tracking **employees** (delivery staff): information duties, consent, limits outside working hours, and staff representatives if any;
   - tracking **rental customers** (later, through Karia): what must be in the rental contract and at vehicle hand-over;
   - individuals tracking a vehicle **also driven by someone else** (family member, borrower).
3. **Retention periods** for location history. Currently 90 days by default, adjustable from 7 to 365. What maximum or minimum applies?
4. **Hosting location** and cross-border transfer if servers are outside Tunisia.
5. **Requests from authorities** for location data: procedure and who may approve.
6. **Data subject rights**: access, correction, deletion; response delays.

## Push notifications
14. With `PUSH_PROVIDER=expo`, alert notifications (type, vehicle name, plate) pass through Expo's servers and then Apple/Google. Check whether this counts as a transfer of personal data abroad and what the privacy notice must say.

## Texts to write
7. Terms of use (placeholder version `placeholder-0`, recorded in the `consents` table at sign-up).
8. Privacy policy, including the commitment "we do not sell or share location data".
9. Cookie notice. The app uses only strictly necessary cookies (session, language). Is a banner needed?
10. Notice templates businesses can give their staff and customers.

## Devices and telecoms
11. Rules for importing, approving and selling GPS trackers and their SIM cards (ANF / INT approval).
12. Confirmation that **no remote vehicle immobilisation** is offered. This is excluded by design and should be stated in the terms.

## Brand
